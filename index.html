STAGE 02 / 05
VERIFIED ENGINE MULTI-THREADED CA SOLVER

Julia 2D Cellular-Automata Flood-Routing Engine

A multi-threaded 2D cellular-automata diffusive flood-routing engine written in Julia. Routes storm surge over Digital Elevation Models using gravity-driven head-gradient flow with strict mass conservation.

AEGIS delegates flood propagation to a dedicated Julia daemon (server.jl). It executes a gravity-driven 2D Cellular Automata (CA) diffusive wave routing scheme over high-resolution DEM grids. Fluid flows between adjacent orthogonal cells only when a positive hydraulic head gradient exists, conditioned by the parameter projection from Stage 1.

\(H_{i,j} = \text{DEM}_{i,j} + \text{Depth}_{i,j}, \quad \Delta V = \min\!\left(\text{Depth}_{i,j},\; \alpha \cdot \sum_k \Delta H_k\right), \quad \text{Flow}_k = \Delta V \cdot \frac{\Delta H_k}{\sum_m \Delta H_m}\)

Stage 05: Human Authorization & Simulated CAP Dispatch

Gemini Flash synthesizes Common Alerting Protocol (CAP) tactical advisories grounded by retrieved SOP context. Simulated dispatch requires human authorization before transmission.

Julia's physical telemetry (node depths, parametric trigger statuses) and the top-2 retrieved SOP chunks are injected into a structured Gemini Flash prompt. The model synthesizes multilingual CAP-compliant advisory drafts with per-node action directives. Human incident commanders review and authorize all dispatches; simulated delivery transmits alerts strictly in test mode. If the API key is unset or all retries exhaust, a deterministic fallback immediately yields a structured advisory draft so operational commanders never see a raw traceback.

DOCUMENTED LIMITATIONS (FROM SCIENTIFIC CRITIQUE)
  • Diffusive vs. Navier-Stokes Scheme: Simplified 2D CA diffusive wave routing captures gravity head equilibrium, but omits dynamic momentum advection and coastal breaker zone wave setup.
  • Tidal Prism Coupling: Does not simulate astronomical spring-tide amplification in the adjacent Chilika lagoon estuary.
  • Micro-Topographic Dune Attenuation: Coastal littoral sand dunes (<1m relief) deflected radar backscatter during satellite passes but are smoothed out in 30m elevation rasters.
These limitations are disclosed in the app's scientific critique panel.
SYSTEM INTEGRITY & BOUNDED TOPOLOGY

Operational Constraints & Phase II Topology

Mission-critical defense systems require definitive operational boundaries over salesmanship. Here is an audited specification of active system boundaries and Phase II topological deployment.

01 · PERCEPTION BOUNDED CONSTRAINT

Synthetic Proxy Ingestion Limitation

Current prototype uses a synthetic proxy tile for V-JEPA evaluation; real satellite ingestion and temporal forecasting are roadmap items. Public Copernicus Sentinel-1 and TerraSAR-X constellations operate on standard orbital revisit intervals of 6 to 12 days.

Operational Boundary: V-JEPA 2 provides learned visual representations that influence physical simulation parameters; it does not perform operational real-time satellite forecasting.
02 · HYDROLOGY QUASI-STATIC

No Dynamic Meteorological Coupling

The Julia CA flood-routing engine assumes a specified boundary hydrograph and rainfall flux. It is not currently coupled in real-time to Doppler weather radar, convective atmospheric micro-physics, or dynamic wind stress waves.

Operational Boundary: Coastal storm surge and inland river head boundaries are provided as parameterized boundary conditions.
03 · RETRIEVAL CONSTRAINED HORIZON

RAG Context Budget Fixed at Top-k = 2

To maintain deterministic latency under 15ms and prevent context window distraction, the vector retriever only passes the top 2 highest-similarity SOP document chunks to the LLM.

Operational Boundary: Peripheral municipal advisories outside the top 2 matches are pruned and must be manually searched.
04 · AUTONOMY ADVISORY ONLY

Zero Autonomous Kinetic Execution

AEGIS generates structured, schema-compliant dispatch orders; it does not trigger physical flood sirens, open dam floodgates, or command drone autopilots without human-in-the-loop authorization.

Operational Boundary: All generated dispatch orders require digital sign-off from an Incident Commander before transmission.

Verified Capabilities vs. Operational Boundaries

Our commitment to rigorous defense: what we claim and what we deliberately bound.

✓ What We Claim (Verified)
  • Real, native Julia 2D CA flood-routing engine (~2–15ms kernel compute).
  • Quantitative 85.6% IoU, 86.2% Precision, 99.3% Recall (≈92.3% F1) backtest against Copernicus EMSR357 radar delineation for Cyclone Fani (Odisha).

  • We do NOT claim operational real-time satellite forecasting or temporal flood prediction hours ahead.
  • We do NOT claim quantitative ground-truth validation for West Bengal or Gujarat (implemented as scenario / stress-test configurations).
  • We do NOT claim "zero hallucination" or autonomous emergency dispatch without human incident commander sign-off.
  • We do NOT claim live orbital satellite streaming without dedicated ground-station telemetry.
  • We do NOT claim centimeter-accurate urban building gutter modeling.
