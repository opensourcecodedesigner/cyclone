# VISAKHAPATNAM DISASTER MANAGEMENT AUTHORITY (VDMA)
## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP) // CYCLONIC STORM SURGE INUNDATION

### 1. POWER & ELECTRICAL INFRASTRUCTURE (DEPARTMENT: POWER)
- **Asset Scope**: Substation Alpha, Harbor Gateway Thermal Switching, 220kV/132kV transmission grids.
- **Trigger Depth**: Water depth > 1.0 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Immediately de-energize primary 220kV step-down transformers to avoid catastrophic flashover and catastrophic grid feedback.
  2. Isolate coastal feeder circuits 4 through 9 and transfer vital hospital telemetry loads to the elevated inland grid.
  3. Deploy high-capacity industrial dewatering submersible pumps (minimum 500 GPM) to switchyard drainage sumps.
  4. Dispatch mobile diesel generator backup (DG sets) on elevated platforms to maintain telemetry communications.

### 2. HEALTHCARE & EMERGENCY MEDICAL FACILITIES (DEPARTMENT: MEDICAL)
- **Asset Scope**: District Hospital Central, Apex Coastal Referral Hospital, Port Clinic.
- **Trigger Depth**: Water depth > 0.5 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Execute vertical evacuation of ICU, Neonatal, and Post-Operative wards from ground floor to Level 2 and above.
  2. Secure liquid medical oxygen (LMO) tank valves and elevate portable emergency oxygen canisters.
  3. Transfer critical life-support systems to uninterruptible power supply (UPS) and rooftop secondary auxiliary generators.
  4. Request NDRF high-clearance amphibious casualty transit for urgent patient relocations to inland university hospital.

### 3. ARTERIAL ROADS & TRANSPORTATION CORRIDORS (DEPARTMENT: TRANSPORT)
- **Asset Scope**: Coastal Trunk Highway Route 16, Southern Marine Logistics Roadway, Inland Evacuation Arterial Highway 5.
- **Trigger Depth**: Water depth > 0.3 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Enforce immediate police barricades and red-flag closure on low-lying littoral highway sectors.
  2. Divert all civilian evacuation traffic and municipal convoys onto Inland Evacuation Arterial Route 9.
  3. Pre-position heavy recovery towing vehicles and earth-moving clearing units at major highway intersections.
  4. Erect physical flood warning LED signs and broadcast radio alerts on Emergency Advisory Frequency 104.4 MHz.
