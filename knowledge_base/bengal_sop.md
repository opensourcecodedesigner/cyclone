# WEST BENGAL DISASTER MANAGEMENT AUTHORITY (WBDMA)
## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP) // CYCLONE SURGE & ESTUARINE INUNDATION
### Operational District: South 24 Parganas & Purba Medinipur Coastal Sector (Digha, Sagar Island, Kakdwip, Haldia)

---

### 1. POWER & ELECTRICAL TRANSMISSION (DEPARTMENT: POWER)
- **Asset Scope**: Digha 33/11kV Coastal Substation, Kakdwip Riverine Switching Station, Sagar Island Estuary Grid Hub, Haldia Industrial Feeder.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.8 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. De-energize 33kV coastal feeder circuits along the Digha sea-wall corridor immediately upon surge alert to prevent ground flashovers.
  2. Isolate vulnerable island transmission links (Sagar Island and Namkhana river crossings) and transfer emergency administrative loads to elevated solar-diesel hybrid microgrids.
  3. Deploy trailer-mounted 750 GPM submersible dewatering pumps to flood-exposed switchyard basements.
  4. Pre-position emergency line restoration teams with high-boom clearing trucks along NH-116B.

---

### 2. HEALTHCARE & EMERGENCY MEDICAL RELIEF (DEPARTMENT: MEDICAL)
- **Asset Scope**: Digha State General Hospital, Sagar Rural Hospital (Island Hub), Contai Sub-Divisional Hospital, Kakdwip Super Speciality Hospital.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.4 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Execute vertical evacuation of ground-floor emergency admissions, neonatology units, and blood banks to Level 2 wards.
  2. Switch life-support apparatus, dialysis suites, and surgical ventilators to rooftop auxiliary diesel generator supply.
  3. Pre-deploy disaster medical kits, antivenom reserves, and water purification sachets to all designated Cyclone Relief Shelters across Sagar and Patharpratima.
  4. Coordinate with Indian Coast Guard and NDRF 2nd Battalion for amphibious hovercraft patient transit across the Muriganga river estuary.

---

### 3. ARTERIAL ROADS, EMBANKMENTS & FERRY CORRIDORS (DEPARTMENT: TRANSPORT)
- **Asset Scope**: NH-116B (Kolkata-Digha Highway), Marine Drive Coastal Bund Road, Kakdwip-Namkhana Ferry Jetty, Diamond Harbour Road.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.3 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Immediate police barricade and heavy-vehicle moratorium on NH-116B seaward lanes between Ramnagar and Digha.
  2. Suspend all passenger ferry operations across Sagar Island, Kakdwip, and Bakkhali jetties; secure pontoon moorings.
  3. Pre-position earth-moving excavators and sandbag breach-control units at critical earthen embankment sectors (Sundarbans Tiger Reserve periphery).
  4. Activate Variable Message Signs (VMS) directing civilian evacuation convoys toward elevated inland shelters at Contai and Diamond Harbour.
