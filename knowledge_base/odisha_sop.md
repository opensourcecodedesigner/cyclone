# ODISHA STATE DISASTER MANAGEMENT AUTHORITY (OSDMA)
## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP) // CYCLONE SURGE & COASTAL FLOODING
### Operational District: Puri Coastal Corridor (Puri Town, Swargadwar, Balukhand, Samuka)

---

### 1. POWER & CRITICAL GRID INFRASTRUCTURE (DEPARTMENT: POWER)
- **Asset Scope**: Samuka Beach Electrical Substation, Balukhand Transformer Yard, Puri Town 33kV Switching Station, Malatipatpur Grid Substation.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.8 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Immediately de-energize primary coastal 33kV step-down transformers at Samuka and Swargadwar beachfront feeders upon surge alert to prevent catastrophic short-circuit flashover.
  2. Isolate shoreline distribution circuits and transfer vital hospital telemetry and municipal pumping loads to the inland Malatipatpur 132kV elevated grid.
  3. Deploy high-capacity industrial dewatering submersible pumps (minimum 500 GPM) to switchyard drainage sumps.
  4. Dispatch mobile diesel generator backup (DG sets) on elevated platforms to maintain telemetry and emergency radio communications.

---

### 2. HEALTHCARE & EMERGENCY MEDICAL RELIEF (DEPARTMENT: MEDICAL)
- **Asset Scope**: Puri District Headquarters Hospital, Swargadwar Emergency Clinic, Gopabandhu Ayurvedic Hospital, Red Cross Cyclone Shelter Pentakota.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.5 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Execute vertical evacuation of ICU, Neonatal, and Post-Operative wards from ground floor to Level 2 and above in District Hospital.
  2. Secure liquid medical oxygen (LMO) tank valves and elevate portable emergency oxygen canisters above projected water line.
  3. Transfer critical life-support systems to uninterruptible power supply (UPS) and rooftop secondary auxiliary generators.
  4. Request NDRF high-clearance amphibious casualty transit for urgent patient relocations to inland Bhubaneswar AIIMS referral hospital.

---

### 3. ARTERIAL ROADS & EVACUATION CORRIDORS (DEPARTMENT: TRANSPORT)
- **Asset Scope**: Swargadwar Coastal Boulevard, Puri-Konark Marine Drive (NH-316), Grand Road (Bada Danda), NH-316 Inland Artery.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.3 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Enforce immediate police barricades and red-flag closure on low-lying Swargadwar coastal boulevard and Puri-Konark Marine Drive.
  2. Divert all civilian evacuation traffic and municipal convoys onto NH-316 Inland Artery towards Bhubaneswar.
  3. Pre-position heavy recovery towing vehicles and earth-moving clearing units at major highway intersections (Malatipatpur bypass).
  4. Erect physical flood warning LED signs and broadcast radio alerts on Emergency Advisory Frequency 104.4 MHz.
