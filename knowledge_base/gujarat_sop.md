# GUJARAT STATE DISASTER MANAGEMENT AUTHORITY (GSDMA)
## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOP) // CYCLONE SURGE & ARABIAN SEA COASTAL THREAT
### Operational District: Kutch & Devbhumi Dwarka Sector (Jakhau Port, Mandvi, Naliya, Mundra)

---

### 1. POWER & CRITICAL INDUSTRIAL GRID (DEPARTMENT: POWER)
- **Asset Scope**: GUJ-PWR-01 (Kutch Lignite Power Station), GETCO 66kV Jakhau Coastal Substation, Mandvi Primary Distribution Yard, Naliya Thermal Feeder, Kandla Port Grid Intertie.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.7 meter (Critical Parametric Threshold).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Immediately isolate 66kV/220kV transmission busbars and activate emergency trip at GUJ-PWR-01 (Kutch Lignite Power Station) upon surge crest exceeding 0.7m to prevent catastrophic short-circuit flashover and salt contamination.
  2. Implement controlled wind turbine feathering and brake-locking across coastal Kutch wind parks exceeding 90 km/h wind gusts.
  3. Pre-stage heavy-duty diesel generators on elevated concrete plinths for Mandvi desalination plant and municipal water reservoirs.
  4. Coordinate rapid electrical circuit re-routing via inland Anjar-Bhuj 220kV transmission corridor.

---

### 2. HEALTHCARE & EMERGENCY TRAUMA FACILITIES (DEPARTMENT: MEDICAL)
- **Asset Scope**: Mandvi Sub-District Hospital, Jakhau Port Primary Health Center, Naliya Community Health Centre, Bhuj Civil Hospital Referral Hub.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.4 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Initiate preemptive transfer of all ICU and neonatal patients from low-lying Mandvi Coastal Hospital to inland multi-specialty facility at Bhuj.
  2. Secure Liquid Medical Oxygen (LMO) cryo-cylinders with steel anchor straps against high-wind displacement.
  3. Mobilize mobile medical disaster relief units (MMUs) equipped with potable water testing kits, cholera vaccines, and emergency trauma dressings to Naliya shelters.
  4. Request Indian Air Force (IAF Naliya Station) and NDRF 6th Battalion air-ambulance standby for casualty airlift from isolated coastal fishing hamlets.

---

### 3. ARTERIAL ROADS, SALT FLATS & PORT APPROACHES (DEPARTMENT: TRANSPORT)
- **Asset Scope**: State Highway 45 (Mandvi-Naliya Artery), Jakhau Port Approach Road, NH-41 Coastal Freight Corridor, Mundra Port Road Link.
- **Trigger Depth**: Flood depth > 0.3 meter (Critical).
- **Mandatory Directives**:
  1. Enforce total vehicular curfew on Jakhau Port Approach Causeway due to high risk of tidal overtopping across surrounding Rann salt marshes.
  2. Reroute all heavy container trucks and civilian evacuation convoys onto elevated State Highway 47 towards inland Bhuj.
  3. Clear highway drainage culverts along SH-45 of desert silt and salt deposits to maximize flood discharge capacity.
  4. Deploy State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) recovery cranes and amphibious ATVs at Mandvi bypass junction.
